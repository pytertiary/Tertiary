PyTertiary · Purchases & refunds

Refund Policy.

Full refunds are available within the conditions below. Eligibility depends on your license type, when you purchased it, and whether you've used it.

There are two license options: Basic ($5 for 30 days) and Ultra ($45 for 300 days). Review the timing and usage requirements before requesting a refund.

Refund eligibility.

These time limits are measured from the date and time of purchase. Both requirements must be met when a used key is involved.

Payment instructions

Paid without your key?

When paying, attach the unique 12-character alphanumeric key provided by the app. If you already paid without it, contact us immediately.

01

Find your payment key

In the PyTertiary app, click I don't have a key. The app provides a unique 12-character code containing letters and numbers. Include it with your payment.

02

If you forgot the key, contact us immediately

Join the official Discord server or message the official support account pytertiary as soon as you notice the missing key.

03

Share the details of your payment

Tell support how much you sent, when you sent it, and any helpful identifying details, such as the Cash App account name or payment reference. Never send passwords or other sensitive financial credentials.

Official support

Need to request a refund?

Contact Tertiary via the official Discord server or directly through the Discord account pytertiary. Include which key you purchased and the payment details needed to locate the transaction.
